OUR PRODUCTS

Explore the quality products we provide to keep premises safe and compliant.

Smoke Detector

Our smoke detectors provide early warning of fires by sensing smoke particles in the air. When smoke is detected, the alarm sounds, allowing occupants to respond promptly and safely evacuate the premises.

Fire Break Glass Unit

Break glass units are used to manually trigger fire alarms or release mechanisms in emergency situations. When the glass is broken, it activates the connected alarm circuit to alert everyone in the building.

Baofeng VHF Radio

The Baofeng VHF radio is a reliable two-way communication device ideal for security teams, emergency responders, and site coordination. It provides clear transmission and reception, ensuring effective communication during fire or safety operations.

Fire Blanket

Fire blankets are designed to extinguish small fires by cutting off the oxygen supply. They are perfect for kitchens, laboratories, and workshops — an essential tool for smothering flames quickly and safely.

Automatic Fire Extinguisher

Automatic fire extinguishers activate themselves when a fire is detected, providing 24/7 protection in closed environments such as engine rooms, server cabinets, and storage areas. They release extinguishing agents automatically upon heat detection.

Manual Gong Bell

The manual gong bell is a mechanical fire alarm device that produces a loud ringing sound when activated. It is commonly used in factories, schools, and buildings where a manual fire alert system is required. Simple, reliable, and effective during emergencies.

First Aid Kit

Our fully equipped first aid kits are designed to provide immediate care during emergencies. Ideal for homes, offices, and workplaces — each kit contains essential medical supplies for treating injuries, burns, or cuts before professional help arrives.

Safety Signages

Durable, visible signs to mark exits, assembly points, and hazards — helps staff and visitors stay safe.

Fire Action Plan

The Fire Action Plan provides clear instructions on what to do in case of a fire emergency. It outlines evacuation routes, emergency contacts, and assembly points to ensure everyone on the premises acts quickly and safely during an emergency. Every workplace and building should display this plan in visible locations.

Fire Hydrant

Fire hydrants provide an accessible water source for firefighting operations. They allow firefighters to connect hoses and deliver high-pressure water directly to fire sites quickly and efficiently.

Fire Alarm Control Panel

The Fire Alarm Control Panel (FACP) serves as the central hub of a fire alarm system, receiving signals from detectors and initiating alarms or suppression systems to alert occupants and emergency responders.

Fire Suppression Systems

Fire suppression systems are designed to automatically detect and extinguish fires using agents like gas, foam, or dry chemicals—protecting critical areas such as server rooms, kitchens, and industrial spaces.

Fire Delivery Hose

The fire delivery hose is designed to withstand high pressure and deliver water or foam to the fire from a hydrant or pump. It’s built for durability and flexibility during firefighting operations.

Fire Ball Extinguisher

The fire ball extinguisher activates automatically when in contact with flames, dispersing extinguishing powder to smother fires instantly. Ideal for unattended areas such as storage rooms and electrical panels.

Fire Hose Reel

Fire hose reels provide a controlled water supply to combat fires at the early stages. They are easy to operate and suitable for most building types, ensuring quick response during emergencies.
