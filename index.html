About Us

FAIZ TECHNOLOGIES was registered on 19th July 2022 under the REGISTRATION OF BUSINESS NAMES ACT (cap, 449 section 14) and later incorporated as a limited company on 24th February 2025. We are a leading national safety solution provider offering risk assessment and integrated safety solutions.

Founded in 2022, we have quickly risen to become an industry team player by insisting on the highest training and safety standards. Our integrity, originality, and professionalism ensure you receive the best service for your safety needs.

We are proud of our distinct culture and strong ethical values that guide how we conduct business and develop positive relationships with customers, investors, and communities we serve.
